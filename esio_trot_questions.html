E. Retell in 4-5 Sentences

Write 4-5 sentences to retell the story. A teacher or parent can choose 0-4 points for each part.

  1. Beginning: Who are the main characters and where do they live?
  2. Problem: What does Mrs Silver want for Alfie?
  3. Plan: What idea does Mr Hoppy create?
  4. Result: What happens after the plan works?
  5. Ending: What happens to Alfie later?
